\section*{Summary}

I am a machine learning engineer with a background in robotics and applied statistics. During my master's degree I built lightweight segmentation models for agricultural robots, which taught me to care about model size and latency as much as accuracy.

After graduating I joined an energy startup as a data scientist. I built the probabilistic load forecasting service that the company still uses to plan battery dispatch for 40 residential energy communities, cutting forecast error by 18\% compared with the previous gradient-boosting baseline. I also set up the team's first CI pipeline and model registry, so new models could be validated and deployed in hours instead of weeks.

Most recently I worked at an industrial automation company on anomaly detection for sensor streams from production lines. I led a team of three, worked closely with plant managers to turn alerts into maintenance actions, and reduced unplanned downtime on the pilot line by 12\%.

I enjoy problems where messy real-world data meets a clear business decision. I am looking for a team that ships models to production, values careful validation, and gives people room to learn.
